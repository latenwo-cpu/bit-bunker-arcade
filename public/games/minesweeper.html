About Minesweeper

Minesweeper is the classic logic puzzle of clearing a grid without detonating a hidden mine. Numbers reveal how many mines are hiding in the surrounding squares — use that information to deduce where it's safe to click.

How to play Minesweeper

Click a square to reveal it. A number tells you how many mines are in the 8 squares around it; right-click (or long-press on mobile) to flag a square you believe hides a mine. Clear every safe square to win.

Tip: Start by clicking near the corners or edges of the grid — they have fewer neighboring squares, which usually gives you cleaner logical deductions early on.

Strategy guide

Start with the numbers that match their number of hidden neighbours, then use flags to mark certain mines. When two numbers overlap, compare them to find safe squares.

A little history

Minesweeper became famous as a built-in game on personal computers in the early 1990s.
