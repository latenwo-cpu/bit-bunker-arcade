About Breakout

Breakout is the classic brick-breaking arcade game. Bounce a ball off your paddle to smash rows of bricks above — clear every brick on the screen without letting the ball fall past your paddle.

How to play

Move your paddle left and right with the arrow keys or by dragging on touch screens to keep the ball in play. The ball bounces off your paddle at an angle based on where it hits — use that to aim at specific bricks.

Tip: Aim for the edges of your paddle rather than the dead center — an edge hit sends the ball off at a sharper angle, which is often the only way to reach bricks tucked into the corners.
