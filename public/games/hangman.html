About Hangman

Hangman is the classic word-guessing game: a mystery word is hidden behind blanks, and you guess one letter at a time before you run out of attempts. Guess the whole word to win.

How to play Hangman

Click a letter to guess it. Correct guesses fill in every matching blank in the word; wrong guesses cost you one of your limited attempts. Guess the full word before your attempts run out.

Tip: Start with common vowels and consonants (E, A, R, S, T) — they appear in the majority of English words and quickly reveal the word's shape.

Strategy guide

Start with common vowels such as E, A and O, then move to frequent consonants like R, S, T and N. Use the pattern of revealed letters to narrow down the word before guessing.

A little history

Hangman is a pencil-and-paper guessing game that was already being played in Victorian times.

More tips for Hangman

  • Open with common vowels such as E, A, O and I, then move on to R, S, T, N and L.
  • Use the word length and the blanks you already have to spot patterns such as -ING or -ED endings.
  • Don't burn a guess on a rare letter like Q or Z until the shape of the word demands it.
  • Once a few letters are in place, think of whole words that fit before you guess another letter.
