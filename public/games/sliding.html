About Sliding Puzzle

Sliding Puzzle is a classic 15-puzzle: numbered tiles sit in a grid with one empty space, and you slide tiles into that gap to put every number back in order as fast as you can.

How to play

Click any tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it into the gap. Reorder every numbered tile from smallest to largest, reading left to right, top to bottom, to solve the puzzle.

Tip: Solve the top row and left column first and leave them alone — locking in the edges first makes the remaining smaller grid much easier to finish.
