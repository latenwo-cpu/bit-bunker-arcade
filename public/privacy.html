Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pixel Arcade ("we," "the site," "the arcade") collects when you play the games on this website or in our Android app, how that information is used, and the choices you have. By using the site or app you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

Pixel Arcade is a free-to-play game site and Android app. We collect the minimum information needed to operate it, keep the games working, and support the free ad-supported model:

CategoryWhat's collected
Device & browser Browser type, operating system, and device type (e.g. mobile vs. desktop), collected automatically when you load a page or open the app. A randomly generated ID is stored on your device so we can count unique players; it is not your name or your hardware ID.
Approximate location A coarse, IP-based location (city/region level, not GPS-precise), derived from your IP address via a public IP lookup service.
Gameplay activity Which games and screens you visit, game start/finish events, results (win/loss/draw, score), and difficulty selected when playing against the computer opponent.
Online multiplayer If you create or join an online multiplayer room, the nickname you enter and your moves in that game are shared in real time with the other player in the room via our database (Firebase/Firestore). Rooms and their data are temporary and are not linked to any account.
In-game chat If you use the chat in an online room, the messages you type are shared with the other player(s) in that room via our database.
Advertising data Ad impressions, clicks and whether an ad was watched or closed, for ads shown on the site and in the app (see Section 3, Advertising). In the Android app, Google AdMob may also collect your device's advertising ID and similar device information to serve and measure ads.
Cloud save (optional) If you choose to back up your progress, we store your game progress (XP, coins, achievements, high scores) linked to a sign-in identity. This is either an anonymous Firebase ID or, on the website, your Google account (we receive the email address of that account from Google).
Notifications (optional) If you turn on reminders in the Android app, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content or schedule is sent to us.

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information to play the games.

2. How we use this information

  • To operate and improve the games (e.g. understanding which games are popular, fixing bugs, and balancing computer-opponent difficulty).
  • To enable real-time online multiplayer between two players who share a room code.
  • To measure ad performance so the site and app can remain free to play.
  • To store the progress you choose to back up with Cloud Save and restore it on a new device.
  • To detect and prevent abuse of the multiplayer system.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising & cookies

Pixel Arcade displays ads to support the cost of running the site and app. Depending on how ads are currently configured, this may include:

  • Google AdSense — Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization settings at google.com/settings/ads, and read Google's own policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google AdMob (Android app) — Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads) and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Directly-sold or house ads — we may show banners, images or short videos that we control ourselves, for example after a game ends. These can be closed with the X button or close on their own, and they do not use third-party tracking cookies.

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or another region with similar requirements, you may be shown a consent prompt for personalized advertising where applicable.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party service providers to operate the site:

  • Firebase (Google) — hosting, real-time database for multiplayer rooms and chat, sign-in for Cloud Save, file storage for ad media, and analytics storage.
  • IP lookup service (ipapi.co) — used to work out an approximate city/country from your IP address for analytics.
  • Google AdSense / Google AdMob — advertising, where enabled (see Section 3).

These providers may process data on servers located outside your country of residence.

5. Data retention

Analytics events are retained for as long as reasonably needed to understand site usage and are periodically purged. Multiplayer room data is not linked to an account.

6. Children's privacy

Pixel Arcade is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, and the Android app is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

7. Your choices

  • You can play every game on this site without entering any personal information.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising via Google's Ads Settings and your browser's cookie settings.
  • You can clear your browser's local storage/cookies, or clear the app's data / uninstall the app, at any time to remove locally-stored game data.
  • You can turn reminder notifications off in the app's settings or in your Android system settings.
  • Deleting your data: to request deletion of your Cloud Save and any analytics linked to your player ID, email us (Section 9) with the ID shown in the app, or the Google account you used, and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@pixelarcade-44662.web.app.
