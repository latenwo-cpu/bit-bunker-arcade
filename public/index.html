About this arcade

Pixel Arcade is a free collection of 21 browser games, built to run instantly with no download, install, or account required. Classic tabletop games like Tic-Tac-Toe, Checkers, Reversi, Connect Four, Dots & Boxes, and Battleship support a local built-in computer opponent with three difficulty levels, as well as pass-and-play and online multiplayer against a friend using a shareable room code. Puzzle and arcade favorites — Snake, Pong, Breakout, Minesweeper, Pixel Invaders, 2048, Sliding Puzzle, Memory Match, Whack-a-Mole, Word Search, Hangman, and Simon Says — are tuned for quick, replayable sessions on desktop or mobile. Three original 3D games (Neon Runner, Turbo Drift, and Neon Blaster) round out the collection with lightweight WebGL gameplay that runs directly in the browser.

Everything on this site is playable for free. No sign-up is required to play; creating an online multiplayer room only needs a nickname. We may show ads to help keep the arcade free to play — see our Privacy Policy for details on data collection and cookies.

    Reminder time

    Installed as the Android app, streak/spin/challenge reminders fire on a daily schedule even with the app fully closed. In a browser tab, they only fire while this tab stays open (foreground or background) — closing the tab entirely stops them. The friend high-score reminder is different either way: it needs to check your friends' live scores, so it only fires while you have the app or tab open (needs the Friends list set up and Firebase configured).

    Accessibility

    🎯 Colorblind-safe piece colors

    Repalettes Checkers, Reversi, and Connect Four to a colorblind-safe orange/blue pair and adds a shape marker to every piece, so turns and moves never depend on telling two hues apart.

     

    🩺 Reduced motion

    Cuts down flashing, pulsing, and pop animations across the arcade — including the Simon Says light flash — for anyone sensitive to motion. Turned on automatically if your device already asks for reduced motion.
