Contact Pixel Arcade

Pixel Arcade is a small independent project, and we read every message. Whether you found a bug, have an idea for a new game or want to ask about your data, we are happy to hear from you.

Email

pixelarcadesupport@gmail.com

What to include

  • The name of the game and what you were doing when the problem happened.
  • Your device and browser (for example, Chrome on Android).
  • A screenshot if you can, and a room code if it was an online match.

Response time

We usually reply within three to five working days.

Privacy and data requests

To see what we store, read our Privacy Policy. To remove your online data, see Delete my data.
