About Pixel Arcade

Pixel Arcade is an independent project that brings classic arcade, puzzle and board games to the browser. Every game runs instantly on phones, tablets and computers, with no download, no installation and no account needed.

What you will find here

The arcade has 28 games: tabletop favourites such as Tic-Tac-Toe, Checkers, Reversi, Connect Four, Dots & Boxes and Battleship, word and puzzle games such as Hangman, Word Search, 2048, Sliding Puzzle, Memory Match and Minesweeper, and arcade games such as Snake, Pong, Breakout, Pixel Invaders and Whack-a-Mole. There are also three neon 3D games: Neon Runner 3D, Turbo Drift 3D and Neon Blaster 3D, plus Bunker Defense, and newer favourites Block Stack, Pixel Flap, Lights Out, Frog Crossing, Ludo and Snakes and Ladders.

How the games work

The games are written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript and run entirely inside your browser. Board games include a computer opponent with three difficulty levels, a pass-and-play mode, and online rooms where you can invite a friend with a share code. Progress such as coins, achievements and high scores is kept on your device and can be backed up if you choose to sign in.

Why it is free

Pixel Arcade is supported by advertising, which pays for hosting and lets us keep adding games. We aim to keep ads clearly separate from gameplay, and we describe the data involved in our Privacy Policy.

Guides for every game

Each game has its own page with rules, tips, strategy and background, so you can learn how to play before you start. If you spot a bug or have an idea for a new game, please get in touch.
