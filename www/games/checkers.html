About Checkers

Checkers (Draughts) is a classic strategy board game played on an 8x8 board. Move your pieces diagonally, jump over your opponent's pieces to capture them, and reach the far row to crown a King with extended movement.

How to play Checkers

Click a piece to see its legal moves, then click a highlighted square to move it. Captures are mandatory where available. Play a friend locally, face the computer at three difficulty levels, or play online with a room code.

Tip: Avoid moving pieces to the edge columns early — edge pieces can only be captured from one direction, but they also have fewer moves available to escape a trap.

Strategy guide

Keep your back row intact as long as possible to stop kings, and trade pieces when you are ahead. Advance in pairs so your pieces protect each other.

A little history

Checkers, also called draughts, descends from board games that are thousands of years old.
