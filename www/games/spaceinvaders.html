About Pixel Invaders

Pixel Invaders is a retro-style arcade shooter inspired by classic alien-invasion games. Waves of pixel invaders descend from the top of the screen — blast them before they reach the bottom.

How to play Pixel Invaders

Use the arrow keys (or on-screen touch controls on mobile) to move your cannon left and right, and press spacebar or tap to fire. Clear each wave of invaders to advance to the next, faster level.

Tip: Shoot from beneath gaps in the invader formation rather than chasing individual targets — it clears more of the wave with fewer wasted shots.

Strategy guide

Clear the outer columns first to slow the descent of the formation. Stay under cover, fire in short bursts, and keep an eye on faster bonus targets.

A little history

The genre was defined by Space Invaders, released by Taito in 1978, which set the template for fixed shooters.

More tips for Pixel Invaders

  • Clear the invaders column by column from the edges to reduce how wide the formation is.
  • Deal with the bottom row first; those are the closest to reaching you.
  • Stay under gaps in the formation, where their shots have the least room to hit you.
  • Fire in short, timed shots instead of holding down the button, so you can dodge between them.
