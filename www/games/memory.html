About Memory Match

Memory Match is a classic card-matching game. Flip two cards at a time to reveal them — if they match, they stay face up; if not, they flip back over. Clear the whole board using the fewest flips possible.

How to play Memory Match

Click any face-down card to reveal it, then click a second card to try to find its match. Matching pairs stay revealed. Play solo for a best-time run, or challenge a friend or online opponent to see who finds the most pairs.

Tip: Mentally map each card's position as you flip it, even on a miss — memory games are won by tracking what you've already seen, not just by luck.

Strategy guide

Start methodically from one corner so you build a mental map of every revealed card. Say the positions of matched symbols quietly to yourself to reinforce them.

A little history

Card-matching memory games, often called Concentration or Pairs, have been played with ordinary card decks for generations.
