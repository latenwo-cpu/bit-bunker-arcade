About Battleship

Battleship is the classic naval strategy game of hidden fleets. Your ships are placed on a grid your opponent can't see, and you take turns calling shots to try to sink each other's fleet first.

How to play Battleship

Take turns clicking a square on the opponent's grid to fire. A hit is marked in red, a miss in white. Sink every ship in the opposing fleet before your own fleet goes down to win.

Tip: After landing a hit, try the squares directly adjacent to it before jumping elsewhere on the grid — ships are always placed in a straight line, so a hit usually means more hits nearby.

Strategy guide

After a hit, probe the four neighbouring squares to find the ship direction. Search in a checkerboard pattern, since no ship is shorter than two squares.

A little history

Battleship began as a pencil-and-paper game played in the early twentieth century before it became a commercial board game.

More tips for Battleship

  • Search in a checkerboard pattern. Every ship covers at least two squares, so you never need to fire at every cell.
  • After a hit, probe the four neighbours, then follow the line until the ship sinks.
  • When you place your fleet, avoid predictable layouts: don't line every ship along the edge, and don't cluster them together.
  • Sink a ship before you go back to searching; loose hits are wasted information.
