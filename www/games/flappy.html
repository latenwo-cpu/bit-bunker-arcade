About Pixel Flap

Pixel Flap is a one-button arcade challenge: tap to flap, glide through the gaps between pipes, and see how far you can get without crashing.

How to play Pixel Flap

Tap the screen, click, or press Space to flap. Gravity pulls you down constantly, so short rhythmic taps keep you level. Each pipe you pass scores one point.

Tip: Don't over-tap. Small, steady taps make the bird easier to control than big panicky ones.

Strategy guide

Aim for the middle of each gap and look at the next pipe, not the bird. The pipes get a little faster as your score climbs.

A little history

One-button arcade games became a mobile phenomenon in the 2010s because anyone can learn them in seconds but few can master them.
