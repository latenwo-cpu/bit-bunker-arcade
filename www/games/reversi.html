About Reversi

Reversi (Othello) is a strategy game of flipping discs. Sandwich your opponent's discs between two of your own to flip them to your color, and win by controlling the majority of the board when it's full.

How to play Reversi

Click any highlighted square to place a disc there; any of your opponent's discs trapped in a straight line between your new disc and another of your discs get flipped to your color. Play locally, vs. computer, or online.

Tip: Corners can never be flipped once captured — prioritizing corner squares over raw disc count early in the game usually pays off by the endgame.

Strategy guide

Corners are the strongest squares because they can never be flipped. Avoid squares next to corners early, and aim for fewer discs in the middle game rather than more.

A little history

Reversi was published in England in the 1880s, and a variant with fixed starting discs later became widely known as Othello.

More tips for Reversi

  • Mobility matters more than disc count in the early game. Having fewer discs and more available moves is a strength.
  • Corners can never be flipped, so grab them whenever you safely can.
  • Avoid the squares next to a corner until the corner is taken, because they hand it to your opponent.
  • Edges are valuable once you can hold them, and risky if you leave gaps.
